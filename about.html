About Excellect

Three shops, one club

Excellect Vape Club is a wholesale and retail vape and hookah shop on 21 Frank Road in Bardene, Boksburg.

01 · Who we are

Find us by the glow

Excellect Vape Club is at Shop B38, B39 & B40 in East Rand Traders Square. We take up three shops: glass hookahs and pipes on lit shelves, rows of e-liquids, and a full wall of shisha flavours.

We sell retail and wholesale, so you can pop in for one bottle of juice or stock up for your own shop. Can't make it in? Order through our delivery partner 7Waves.
