Ordering, delivery & the fine print

How to order from us, how 7Waves delivery works, and straight answers to what people ask at the counter.

01 · How to order

Three easy steps

Fill your bag

Browse the shop and add what you want. Everything listed is in stock.

Send it on WhatsApp

Open your bag, pick Collect or 7Waves, and tap send. Your order arrives as a message to us.

We confirm, you pay

We check everything is packed, confirm the total and sort out payment with you. Then it's yours.

03 · FAQs

Asked at the counter

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp or call +27 84 386 9600.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through our delivery partner 7Waves. Choose "7Waves" in your bag, copy your list and place the order on 7Waves. Or collect from the shop.

What is 7Waves?

7Waves is our delivery partner for fast local orders. Pick "7Waves" in your bag, copy your list and order the same items on 7Waves.

Can I order online and collect in the shop?

Yes. Choose "Collect in Bardene, Boksburg" in your bag. We'll let you know on WhatsApp when it's packed and waiting for you at East Rand Traders Square.

How do I pay?

Pay in the shop when you collect. If you order on WhatsApp, we'll confirm the total and how to pay before anything is charged.

Is everything on the website really in stock?

The website only lists products that are in stock on our stock list. Things do sell fast, so if something goes before we confirm your order, we'll tell you straight away and suggest something similar.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes. We only sell to adults and check ID in the shop and on collection.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes, we're a wholesale and retail store. Send us your list on WhatsApp for bulk prices.

My device stopped working. What now?

Message us with your order details and what's happening. Warranty depends on the brand, but we'll help you sort it out.

Do you sell hookah coals and accessories?

Yes: coals, heads, hoses, tongs and more. Have a look in Hookah.

Can you get a product you don't have?

Often, yes. Tell us what you're after and we'll check with our suppliers.
